How it works

Three steps. Completely invisible.

Works in the background while you interview. No suspicious activity, no window switching, no browser tabs.

1

Press a hidden hotkey

A discreet key combination captures your screen instantly. Intercepted at the OS level - completely invisible to any screen-monitoring software or browser.

2

AI analyses the problem

Your screenshot is sent to Claude AI which reads the problem, detects the language, and generates a well-reasoned solution with explanation.

3

Answer appears on your phone

Results stream to your phone in seconds. Glance down naturally. The interviewer sees you thinking. You're reading the solution.
