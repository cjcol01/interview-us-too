02  ·  How it works

      
Two ways to capture. One silent result.

    

    

      

        

          
1

          

        

        
Tap or hold a hidden hotkey

        
Tap to capture your screen instantly — intercepted at the OS level, invisible to monitoring software. Hold while the interviewer speaks to send their words to AI.

      

      

        

          
2

          

        

        
AI reads or listens

        
Claude reads the problem, detects the language, and generates a solution with complexity analysis. For voice, Whisper transcribes first — same output, hands-free.

      

      

        

          
3

          

        

        
Answer streams to your phone

        
Results appear in seconds with syntax-highlighted code and explanation. Missed something? Instant replay captures the last 15 seconds of speech.

      

    

    

      

        
      

    

  







  

    

      
03  ·  Features

      
Built for the moment your brain stalls.

    

    


      

        

          
        

        
Undetectable by design

        
Hotkeys are intercepted at the OS level — not inside the browser. No window switching, no tabs, no clipboard access. Screen-monitoring software sees nothing unusual because nothing unusual is happening.

        
OS-level interception · Zero browser footprint

      


      

        

          
        

        
Streams to your phone

        
The answer never appears on your interview screen. Syntax-highlighted code, complexity, and explanation — formatted to read at a glance. Also works on a second monitor.

      


      

        

          
        

        
Screen or voice

        
Tap to screenshot the question, or hold to record the interviewer. Both stream the same Claude analysis.

      


      

        

          
        

        
Instant replay

        
Missed what they said? Replay sends the last 15s of their question to AI after the fact.
