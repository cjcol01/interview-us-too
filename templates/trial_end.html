You've seen how it works. Before you decide what's next, here are a couple of things worth knowing.

Pin the extension

Click the puzzle piece icon in the top right of Chrome, find InterviewAce, and click the pin icon to keep it visible in your toolbar.

You don't have to do this, but it lets you know at a glance whether it's running.

Tune the response detail

The + / − buttons on the monitor page control how detailed the AI's answers are — fewer dots for a quick nudge, more dots for a full walkthrough.

Useful if the default responses feel too brief or too verbose for your style.

Keep the monitor off the interviewer's screen

Open the monitor page on your phone or a second device — not on the same screen the interviewer can see. The extension captures from your main computer; the hints appear wherever you're logged in.

Most people prop their phone out of frame or use a second monitor positioned behind the camera.
