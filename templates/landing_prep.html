02  ·  How it works

      
Two ways to capture. One instant result.

      
Works in the background while you practice. No clunky notes doc, no tab-flipping, no losing your train of thought.

    


    

      

        

          
1

          

        

        
Tap a hotkey

        
Tap to capture your screen instantly - registered as a background OS shortcut, so your hands never leave the keyboard. Tap the voice key while your mock interviewer speaks, then any key when they finish, to send their words straight to AI.

        
rebind all three hotkeys to anything you like

      

      

        

          
2

          

        

        
AI reads or listens

        
For screenshots, Claude reads the problem, detects the language, and generates a solution with complexity analysis. For voice, Whisper transcribes first, then Claude reasons through it - same output, hands-free input.

        
auto language detection · adjustable complexity

      

      

        

          
3

          

        

        
Answer streams to your browser

        
Results appear in seconds with syntax-highlighted code and explanation. Miss something? Hit instant replay to capture and send the last 15 seconds of speech to AI.

        
phone, second monitor or laptop

      

    


    

      

        
      


      

        

          

            
              
            
            
Transparent by design

          

          
A screen-share warning banner fires the moment the assistant runs during a live session - keeping every practice run above-board. Hotkeys are registered as background OS shortcuts, so your hands never leave the keyboard during drills.

          
built-in screen-share warning · interview-safe by default

        


        

          

            
              
            
            
Streams to your phone

          

          
Coaching never clutters your practice screen. Syntax-highlighted code, complexity, and explanation - formatted to read at a glance. Also works on a second monitor or laptop.
