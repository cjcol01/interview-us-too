How it works

Two ways to capture.
One instant result.

Works in the background while you practice. No clunky notes doc, no tab-flipping, no losing your train of thought.

1

Tap or hold a hotkey

Tap to capture your screen instantly — registered as a background OS shortcut, so your hands never leave the keyboard. Hold while your mock interviewer speaks and release to send their words straight to AI.

2

AI reads or listens

For screenshots, Claude reads the problem, detects the language, and generates a solution with complexity analysis. For voice, Whisper transcribes first, then Claude reasons through it — same output, hands-free input.

3

Answer streams to your browser

Results appear in seconds with syntax-highlighted code and explanation. Miss something? Hit instant replay to capture and send the last 15 seconds of speech to AI.
