🔁 Instant replay — lock to your tab first

Before your interview, click the InterviewAce extension icon while on the tab you want to share audio from (normally your meeting tab) and press Lock to tab. This continuously buffers audio from that tab. During the interview, press the replay hotkey to instantly send the last few seconds for AI analysis — no re-recording needed.

⌨️ Typing mode & passthrough

Press the typing hotkey to enter typing mode — type your question silently, then press it again or hit Enter to submit.
