Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about acing your next interview with InterviewAce.

Product & how it works

What is InterviewAce?›

InterviewAce is a real-time AI interview assistant. It watches your screen during a live interview, instantly understands each question, and streams a clear, structured answer directly to your browser dashboard so you always know exactly what to say.

How does InterviewAce work?›

Install the Chrome extension and open your InterviewAce dashboard. When a question appears on screen (or is spoken aloud), press the capture hotkey. The extension takes a screenshot and sends it to our server, where Claude's vision AI reads the question and streams a tailored answer back to your dashboard in seconds.

Why real-time help instead of just preparing beforehand?›

No amount of preparation covers every question an interviewer will throw at you. Real-time assistance is like having an expert co-pilot in your ear. It bridges the gap between what you've studied and what actually comes up in the room. You still drive; we just make sure you're never caught off-guard.

Who is InterviewAce for?›

Anyone going through a high-stakes interview. That includes software engineers facing coding and system-design rounds, job-seekers preparing for behavioural interviews, and professionals in any field who want an extra edge in virtual meetings. If you interview over a screen, InterviewAce can help.

What kinds of interviews does it support?›

Any interview that happens on a screen. That includes job interviews across all industries and roles, academic admissions calls, sales conversations, client pitches, and any other high-stakes discussion where having a well-structured answer matters. Whether the question is on a shared problem set, a slide deck, or just spoken aloud, InterviewAce adapts.

Which AI model powers the answers?›

Answers are generated by Claude (Anthropic), one of the most capable AI models available. Claude can see your screen via screenshot capture, read the full content on it, and listen to spoken questions via Whisper audio transcription, giving it everything it needs to understand exactly what is being asked.

How accurate are the answers?›

Claude is highly accurate on standard interview topics: algorithms, data structures, system design, and common behavioural frameworks. Like any AI, it can occasionally make mistakes on very niche or ambiguous questions, so always apply your own judgement before speaking. We recommend using the answers as a strong starting point, not a script to read verbatim.

Using InterviewAce

What do I need to install?›

Just the InterviewAce Chrome extension. Once installed, it runs in the background on your existing browser. No separate app, no virtual machine. Pair it with your dashboard at interviewace.co/app and you're ready to go.

Which platforms and apps does it work with?›

InterviewAce works with any browser-based content: Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, HackerRank, CoderPad, LeetCode, and most other coding or video-interview platforms. If it appears in Chrome, the extension can capture it.

Can I use it alongside my phone?›

Yes. During onboarding, scan the QR code shown on screen with your phone. This opens your InterviewAce dashboard on mobile, so you can read AI answers on your phone while keeping your computer screen interview-clean. Many users find this the most natural setup.

Does it work with spoken / audio questions?›

Yes. In addition to screenshot capture, InterviewAce includes an audio capture mode powered by OpenAI Whisper. Press the audio hotkey and speak (or replay the interviewer's question); it will transcribe the speech and generate an answer just as it does for screen captures.

What is a "session" and how long does it last?›

A session is a timed block of active interview assistance. Paid sessions last 2.5 hours, enough for even the longest interview loop. Trial sessions last 10 minutes so you can see the product in action before you commit. Sessions are deducted from your balance when you start them, and they expire at the end of their window whether or not you use all the time.

How do I start a session?›

From your dashboard, click Start session. The timer begins immediately. Make sure you start your session shortly before your interview (not hours beforehand) so the 2.5-hour window covers the whole conversation.

Privacy & discretion

How discreet is InterviewAce during an interview?›

InterviewAce runs in your browser and displays answers on a separate dashboard tab (or your phone). It does not inject anything into your video feed, your audio, or your shared screen. As long as you keep the dashboard tab off your shared screen, the interviewer will not see it. Note: like any browser window, the dashboard can be captured if you share your entire screen, so only share the specific window or tab you want the interviewer to see.

Is my data private and secure?›

All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored encrypted at rest. We never sell your data to third parties, and we do not use your interview content to train AI models. Your screenshots and answers are processed by Anthropic's Claude API under their privacy policy; they similarly do not train on API inputs.

Do you store my screenshots or interview content?›

Screenshots are sent to Claude for processing and are not permanently stored on our servers. Session metadata (start/end time, usage counts) is stored on your account for billing purposes. We don't build a library of your interview questions or answers.
